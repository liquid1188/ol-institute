Annual Retreat Cycle

Three retreats through the year

  1. February/March

    Great Lent Retreat

    • Repentance, Reconciliation, and Renewal
    • 3-day silent retreat option

  2. June/July

    Post-Pentecost Retreat

    • Theme: Life in the Spirit
    • Focus on prayer of the heart
    • Hesychast tradition workshops

  3. November/December

    Nativity Fast Retreat

    • Theme: Incarnation and Theosis
    • Family retreat track available
    • Advent preparation

Study Pilgrimages

The next step in formation

For students and retreatants, a pilgrimage is the hands-on part of the course. The sites studied in class and the saints met in prayer become places to stand, to pray the liturgy, and to learn on the ground.

  1. Prepare

    Readings and sessions drawn from the Institute’s programs before departure

  2. Encounter

    Teaching at the sites, with prayer and liturgy along the way

  3. Reflect

    Time together afterward to bring the experience back into study and parish life

Annual OL Pilgrimages

  1. Year 1

    Holy Land

    Jerusalem, Bethlehem, Jordan River

  2. Year 2

    Byzantine Heritage

    Constantinople, Cappadocia, Greece

  3. Year 3

    Ancient Christianity

    Egypt, Syria, Rome

The Orientale Lumen Foundation continues its own ecumenical pilgrimages, open to all who wish to travel and pray with Catholics and Orthodox. Institute pilgrimages build on that tradition as a part of study and formation.
