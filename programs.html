Our flagship degree

Master of Arts in Ecumenical Studies

A master’s degree in ecumenical studies is rare anywhere in the world, and this is the first in the United States. Where ecumenism is taught at all, it usually sits inside a single church’s school. The OL Institute is different. It grows out of three decades of Orientale Lumen, where Catholic and Orthodox bishops, scholars and laypeople have prayed and studied side by side. Here the Catholic-Orthodox dialogue is the subject itself, taught by people who have spent their lives in it.

Graduate degree2 Years36 credit hours

In the program

  • Explore the history of inter-religious and ecumenical dialogue
  • Critically understand the process of inter-religious and ecumenical dialogue
  • Learn the history of the Catholic-Orthodox relationship
  • Investigate the status of Orthodox-Catholic dialogues

Graduate Programs

Three more master’s degrees join the MA in Ecumenical Studies.

Graduate Programs offer in-depth studies and professional/academic preparation, which can lead to direct employment, ordination, or a doctoral degree.

2 Years36 credit hours

Master of Arts in Christian Spirituality (MAS)

  • Critically understand the development of Eastern/Western Christian spiritual traditions
  • Explore contemporary Christian spiritual belief and practice
  • Connect spirituality to discernment of personal, professional and vocational questions
  • Develop an understanding of spiritual ecumenism

Master of Theological Studies (MTS)

  • Research-oriented degree
  • Comparative theology emphasis
  • Specializations in: Patristics, Trinitarian Theology, Christology, Pneumatology, Eschatology, Sacramental Theology, etc.

Master of Biblical Studies (MBS)

  • Research-oriented degree
  • Emphasis on comparative biblical analysis
  • Specializations in: Old Testament, New Testament, and Integration with Church History and/or Patristics

Diploma Programs

Diploma Programs offer more comprehensive studies and professional preparation, which can lead to direct employment or an undergraduate degree.

2 Years36 credit hours

Diploma in Spirituality

  • Exploring core content about the historical similarities and differences in the various Christian spiritualities
  • Parish teaching qualification and/or preparation for ministerial leadership

Diploma in Comparative Theological Studies

  • Broad-based theological education in the Catholic and Orthodox contexts
  • Parish teaching qualification and/or preparation for ministerial leadership

Diploma in Biblical Studies

  • Comparative, broad-based biblical education
  • Parish teaching qualification and/or preparation for youth and family catechesis

Diploma in Ecumenical Studies

  • History of Orthodox and Catholic Relations
  • Contemporary synthesis of East-West cultural distinctions
